# Stakeholder Analysis

## Purpose

This document identifies the key stakeholders involved in the Capital Markets Regulatory Reference Data Change project and defines how they should be engaged throughout delivery.

Effective stakeholder management is particularly important because the project requires coordination across product, business, technical, operational and external financial institution stakeholders.

## Project Manager's Role in Stakeholder Coordination

The Project Manager does not replace the specialist responsibilities of Product, Business Analysis, Development or QA.

The PM's role is to ensure that:

- Required stakeholders are involved at the appropriate time
- Actions have clear owners and target dates
- Decisions are recorded and communicated
- Dependencies between teams are visible
- Blockers are followed through to resolution
- Risks and issues are escalated appropriately
- Changes affecting scope, schedule or delivery are assessed
- Stakeholders receive appropriate project-status information

For example, if an external financial institution raises a requirement that conflicts with the agreed specification, the PM would coordinate the relevant Product, BA and technical stakeholders to assess the request rather than independently deciding the technical solution.

## Stakeholder Management Principles

The project will follow five stakeholder management principles:

1. **Right people, right time** — involve stakeholders when their input or decision is required.
2. **Clear ownership** — every material action should have an identified owner and target date.
3. **Transparent status** — risks, issues and delays should be communicated early.
4. **Decision traceability** — significant decisions should be recorded.
5. **Escalation by impact** — issues should be escalated when they threaten scope, schedule, quality or the regulatory deadline.
