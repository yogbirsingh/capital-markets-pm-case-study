# Project Retrospective and Closure

## Project Outcome

**Project Status: Complete**

The regulatory reference data change was successfully delivered to production during the planned Week 10 release window.

Post-release validation was completed successfully, with no Critical or High production issues identified.

The project proceeded to closure in Week 11.

## Delivery Summary

The project successfully delivered:

- Approved regulatory reference-data changes
- Supplementary reference-data field introduced through CR-01
- System and integration testing
- External stakeholder UAT
- Production deployment
- Post-release validation

The original Week 10 production-release milestone was achieved.

## What Went Well

- Requirements were approved before development progressed
- RAID items were actively tracked throughout delivery
- CR-01 was formally assessed before being accepted into scope
- The additional scope was accommodated without moving the release date
- UAT stakeholder availability issue I-01 was resolved within the planned testing window
- Business acceptance was obtained before release
- Production deployment and post release validation completed successfully

## Challenges

### CR-01 – Scope Change

An additional requirement was requested after the original requirements had been approved.

The change reduced available schedule contingency but was successfully incorporated following impact assessment.

### I-01 – UAT Stakeholder Availability

The primary external UAT representative became unavailable during the planned UAT window.

A backup participant was coordinated and the affected scenarios were completed within Week 8.

## Lessons Learned

| Lesson | Future Action |
|---|---|
| External stakeholder availability should be confirmed early | Identify primary and backup UAT participants during planning |
| Late scope changes reduce delivery contingency | Protect contingency and apply formal change control |
| Early technical assessment supports reliable planning | Involve technical representatives during requirements analysis |
| Clear UAT entry criteria reduce testing delays | Agree and communicate entry criteria before the UAT window |
| Regular RAID reviews support early intervention | Continue scheduled RAID reviews throughout delivery |
